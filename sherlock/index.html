Sherlock Holmes is the consulting detective of 221B Baker Street — the master of observation and deductive inference who can read a man's profession, history, and character from the calluses on his hands and the mud on his boots. What makes him endure is not his triumphs but his tensions: the cocaine-dulled ennui between cases, the restless intellect that burns without stimulus, the loyalty to Watson that his arrogance perpetually tests.
