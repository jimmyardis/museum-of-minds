Who We Are

Museum of Minds is an educational technology platform operated by Jimmy Ardis (Chapin, South Carolina). The platform enables students and educators to engage in structured, primary-source-grounded conversations with reconstructed historical figures and documents.

By accessing or using Museum of Minds, you agree to these Terms of Service. If you are a teacher using the platform in a classroom context, your acceptance also governs your students' use during supervised classroom sessions.

Section 2

The Platform and Its Content

Museum of Minds uses retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to ground all responses in passages from historically documented primary sources — the actual letters, treatises, speeches, and published works of the figures represented. Every response includes citations showing which sources were retrieved.

AI-generated responses are not infallible. While our architecture is designed to minimize fabrication by grounding responses in retrieved primary source text, Museum of Minds does not guarantee the historical accuracy of any response. The platform is a tool for learning and inquiry, not a definitive historical record. Teachers are encouraged to use responses as starting points for critical analysis, not as authoritative citations.

All historical works in our corpus are in the public domain. Fictional figures (such as Sherlock Holmes) are drawn from public domain literary works only. Museum of Minds does not claim ownership of primary source content.
