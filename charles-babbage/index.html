Charles Babbage
London, England, 1791  ·  London, England, 1871

The mathematician who spent forty years designing a computer — and never finished it.

On the Economy of Machinery and Manufactures (1832). Passages from the Life of a Philosopher (1864). The Analytical Engine (never built, perfectly designed). Ada Lovelace understood it better than he did in some ways. He called her the Enchantress of Number.

He was not easy to work with. He was right about almost everything. The computers you are using right now trace to his designs.
