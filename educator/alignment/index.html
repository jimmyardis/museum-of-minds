Section 3

SC AI Standards Framework Alignment

Framework authored by Andrew Cook, SCDOE Education Associate. Museum of Minds was designed with this framework in mind: the platform is not only a tool that uses AI, it is a demonstration of responsible AI use that students can observe and interrogate.

SC AI Theme How Museum of Minds Demonstrates It
Computing Systems Students witness a real-world AI system (RAG — retrieval-augmented generation) operating transparently: retrieve → ground → generate. The architecture is visible, not hidden behind a consumer interface.
Data and Analytics Source citation on every response teaches data provenance literacy — where did this claim come from, and how reliable is that source? The Trust Score (0–100 corpus depth rating) teaches confidence calibration. Students learn to ask "what is this AI's source?" rather than accepting outputs uncritically.
Algorithms and Programming The pipeline is pedagogically visible: student question → semantic search of 106,000+ primary source passages → grounded AI response. Students can observe the algorithm at work. The How It Works section of the platform walks through each stage explicitly.
Impacts of Computing Demonstrates responsible AI use in a discipline where misinformation is especially consequential — history. Students see how grounding in primary sources constrains AI behavior and why that constraint matters for truth and civic knowledge.
Security and Privacy Architectural data minimization: no PII collected, conversation data auto-deleted, no student accounts required at the classroom level. The platform models privacy-by-design rather than privacy-by-policy.

Section 6

Privacy & Data Posture

No Student PII Collected
Architectural, not policy. No student accounts, no name/email fields, no tracking. Privacy is built into the infrastructure, not enforced by a policy that could be changed.
No AI Training on Conversations
Conversation data is not retained for model training. Student interactions do not improve or inform the underlying AI models in any way.
Conversation Auto-Deletion
Session data is not persisted beyond the active session at the classroom tier. No conversation logs are stored on our servers after the session ends.
FERPA-Aligned
No student education records are created or maintained. The platform cannot become subject to a FERPA request because no student records exist to request.
COPPA-Aligned
No data is collected from users under 13. The architectural no-PII posture applies equally to younger students.
No Student-to-Student Communication
Compliant with SC Bill 3244 expectations for school-device software approval. No social or communication features exist on the platform.
