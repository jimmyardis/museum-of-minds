Data & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Museum of Minds is built for classrooms. That means our approach to data is simple: we collect as little as possible, we hold it briefly, and we never use it for anything other than making the product work.

Effective date: May 18, 2026  ·  museumofminds.com
The Short Version

What You Need to Know in 30 Seconds

Students do not create accounts. No name, email address, or other personally identifiable information is collected from any user of this platform. Conversations auto-delete after 90 days. We do not sell, share, or monetize any user data.

If you are a district administrator evaluating this platform for FERPA compliance, our architecture is designed to remain outside FERPA's scope entirely: because we never hold education records, we are not an educational agency or institution, and we do not act as a "school official" under FERPA. No student-level data of any kind is collected, retained, or transmitted.

Section 1

What We Collect

Museum of Minds collects only what is technically necessary to operate the platform. Specifically:

  • Conversation text. When a user types a message and receives a response, that exchange is held in temporary server memory for the duration of the session. It is used solely to maintain conversational context (so the historical figure remembers what was said earlier in the same session). It is not associated with any user identifier.
  • Session identifiers. A randomly generated session ID is used to link messages within a single conversation. This ID is not tied to a person, a device, or an IP address in any persistent way.
  • Aggregate usage counts. We count the number of conversations started and messages sent, in aggregate, to understand platform load. These counts contain no user-level information.

We do not collect: names, email addresses, school names, grade levels, IP addresses stored beyond a request, device identifiers, location data, or any information about the individual using the platform.

Section 2

Student Access and Classroom Use

In classroom pilots, students access the platform through a classroom passcode provided by their teacher. No student account is created. Students do not enter their name, student ID, school name, or any other identifying information.

Teachers who participate in a pilot program receive access to an aggregate dashboard showing engagement metrics for their classroom as a whole — for example, how many conversations were started, which historical figures were consulted, and how long sessions typically ran. This dashboard contains no student-level data. Individual students are not identifiable from any data we hold.

Section 3

Data Retention

Conversation data (the text of messages sent and received) is automatically deleted after 90 days. This deletion is unconditional — it applies to all sessions regardless of whether a pilot is active, ended, or ongoing.

If a district or teacher ends a pilot early and requests immediate deletion, we will confirm deletion in writing within five business days.

Section 4

Cookies and Tracking

Museum of Minds does not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking, or third-party analytics scripts. We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any comparable tracking technology.

The only browser storage we use is a session cookie or local storage item to maintain conversational context within a single visit. This item expires when the browser tab is closed or at session end.

Section 5

Third-Party Services

Museum of Minds uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Anthropic (Claude API). Conversation messages are sent to Anthropic's API to generate responses. Anthropic's data processing practices are governed by their enterprise API privacy policy. API data is not used by Anthropic to train models unless separately agreed in writing.
  • Pinecone. Our vector database, used to retrieve passages from historical primary sources. Only text excerpts from public-domain historical documents are stored here. No user data is stored in Pinecone.
  • Railway. Our cloud hosting provider. Server logs may include IP addresses in transit; these are not retained or linked to user sessions.

We do not share any user data — including conversation text — with any third party for advertising, marketing, analytics, or any purpose other than operating the platform.

Section 6

FERPA, COPPA, and State Privacy Laws

FERPA. Because Museum of Minds does not collect or maintain education records as defined by FERPA (20 U.S.C. § 1232g), FERPA's requirements regarding consent, access, and disclosure do not apply to our platform in the traditional sense. We are not a party to any student-school record relationship. Districts may nonetheless reference our data practices in their own FERPA compliance documentation.

COPPA. Museum of Minds is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because we collect no personal information from any user, COPPA's verifiable parental consent requirements are not triggered. Classroom use is under the supervision of a teacher who has accepted our Terms of Service.

State laws. We are aware of student data privacy laws in South Carolina and other states. Our no-PII-by-design architecture is intended to remain compliant with these laws as a matter of principle, not just legal obligation.
